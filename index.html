Current Focus

3D EM Organelle Segmentation

Benchmark-driven segmentation research on mitochondria, nuclei, and pan-organelle structures in MitoEM 2.0 and CellMap-style settings.

Research Vision

My long-term goal is to develop generalizable AI systems for 3D biomedical image understanding. Current segmentation models often require dense expert annotation and struggle with domain shift, complex topology, and large-scale volume EM data. My work addresses these challenges by combining label-efficient learning, structural priors, and benchmark-driven evaluation to build reliable tools for scalable biological discovery.

Research Projects

MitoEM 2.0 / MitoVerse

Problem: Existing 3D EM datasets are limited for robust mitochondria instance segmentation and foundation-model benchmarking.

Method: Built a challenging benchmark pipeline with consistent evaluation and model-oriented task design.

Contribution: Provides a standardized testbed for scalable, reproducible mitochondria segmentation research.

PaperBenchmark Context

TopoSlot / RAHSeg / RalphUNet

Problem: Pan-organelle EM segmentation often fails on topologically complex and tightly coupled structures.

Method: Structured conditional segmentation with topology- and affinity-aware constraints.

Contribution: Improves structural consistency and robustness in challenging volume EM cases.

Related Publications

MitoSDT / Structure-Aware Instance Refinement

Problem: Tubular organelles are prone to false merges and disconnections in automatic instance segmentation.

Method: Skeleton-distance and geometry priors with point-cloud-aware refinement modules.

Contribution: Reduces merge errors and preserves biologically meaningful morphology.

Related Manuscript

CR-GLoCo / CVCL

Problem: Expert labeling cost blocks broad deployment of 3D medical segmentation models.

Method: Semi-supervised cross-resolution consistency learning and voxel-wise contrastive learning.

Contribution: Demonstrates strong label efficiency for multi-organ and cross-domain segmentation.
